Yuji Itadori

Yuji Itadori is the main character in the Jujutsu Kaisen manga/anime.

He is introduced as a member of the occultism club of his highschool at Sendai along with two other school partners, as well as an insanely athletic and physically strong person. As a member of his club, he finds a Sukuna finger and after some circumstances, he ends up eating that finger, and as a result, becoming Sukuna's vessel.

Itadori at the beginning lacks a cursed technique, and his only specialty is being outstandingly physically strong. During the story, he develops a "divergent punch", which allows him to attack an opponent with two impacts when hitting them, the second with some lag, allowing him to confuse and increase the damage to the enemy. Apart from that, due to his link with souls as Sukuna's vessel, he manages to make his punches soul renders, hitting the enemy's soul with precision.
He also manages to use Sukuna's cursed techniques since being his vessel "printed" those abilities into his body, as well as he learns to use "Simple domain", "Reverse cursed technique", and "blood manipulation" - the latter by consuming six of the Cursed Womb Death Paintings.

Near the end of the series, Itadori unlocks an unnamed Domain Expansion, which allows him to sure-hit the enemy with his soul-rendering attacks. The domain appears to be a replica of Itadori's childhood town - not Sendai.
