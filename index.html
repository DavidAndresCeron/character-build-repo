Yuji Itadori

Yuji Itadori is the main character in the Jujutsu Kaisen manga/anime.

He is introduced as a member of the occultism club of his highschool at Sendai, along with two other school partners, as well as an incredibly athletic and physically strong person.
As a member of his club, he finds a Sukuna finger, and after some circumstances, he ends up eating that finger, and as a result, becoming Sukuna's vessel. He then joins the first year class of the Tokyo Jujutsu High.

Itadori at the beginning lacks a cursed technique, and his only specialty is being outstandingly physically strong. During the story, he develops a "divergent punch", which allows him to attack an opponent with two impacts when hitting them, the second one with some lag, allowing him to confuse and increase the damage to the enemy. Apart from that, due to his link with souls as Sukuna's vessel, he manages to make his punches soul renders, hitting the enemy's soul with precision.
He also manages to use Sukuna's cursed techniques, since being his vessel "printed" those abilities into his body, as well as he learns to use "Simple domain", "Reverse cursed technique", and "Blood Manipulation" - the latter by consuming six of the Cursed Womb Death Paintings.

Near the end of the series, Itadori unlocks an unnamed "Domain Expansion", which allows him to sure-hit the enemy with his soul-rendering attacks. The domain appears to be a replica of Itadori's childhood town - not Sendai.

Megumi Fushiguro

Megumi Fushiguro is one of the main characters in the Jujutsu Kaisen manga/anime.

He is introduced as someone who is looking for the missing Sukuna finger that Itadori took, and when Fushiguro meets him, he understands the situation and rushes to save Itadori's partners from the curses that will be attracted by the finger's energy. After unsuccessfully retaking the finger from a curse, and subsequent Itadori's transformation, he is saved by his profesor Satoru Gojo, and later becomes one of the students of the first year class of the Tokyo Jujutsu High.

His cursed technique is the "Ten Shadows", an inherited technique from the Zenin clan - to which Fushiguro is related through his deceased father - that allows Fushiguro to summon cursed energy creatures to serve him, and use shadows as a medium of transportation, hiding spot, and/or inventory. The Ten Shadows include the creature known as "Mahoraga", a creature so powerful that no prior user of the technique has managed to tame, as it has the capacity to adapt and become invulnerable to any cursed technique.

Fushiguro manages to develop an incomplete domain expansion known as the "Chimera Shadow Garden", which due to the fact of being incomplete, requires Fushiguro to use a real enclosed space for its execution, and lacks as well a sure-hit effect, being useful only for upgrading his cursed energy usage performance.

Nobara Kugisaki

Nobara Kugisaki is one of the main characters during the first half of the Jujutsu Kaisen manga/anime.

She is introduced as the third member of the first year Tokyo Jujutsu High class, someone from the country side, who at first, judges his other two classmates very superficially and narcissistically, but later ends up caring for them and working hard to be a proficient sorcerer.

Her cursed technique, "Straw Doll", allows her to hit the enemy's soul with the usage of nails, a hammer, and a straw doll, which serve as a medium for Nobara to hit the enemy fom afar.
Her technique manages to be highly effective against enemies who play with their's or others' soul, as it distinguishes the enemy's soul and exclusively targets with lethal blows.

Yuta Okkotsu

Yuta Okkotsu is the main character in the Jujutsu Kaisen 0 prequel, and one of the main characters during the second half of the Jujutsu Kaisen manga/anime.

He is introduced as a weak boy who is stalked by the ghost of his childhood girlfriend, and later has to find the way to exorcise her, and become a jujutsu sorcerer in the process.
After Jujutsu Kaisen 0, he is absent from the plot up until the end of the Shibuya Incident arch, after which he is reintroduced as the executioner of Yuji Itadori in order to kill Sukuna, who is living inside of him.

He is initially equipped with a katana, which serves as a way to connect his cursed energy with Rika Orimoto - his deceased girlfriend - and, later, manages to awaken the "Copy" technique, which allows him to copy the cursed techniques of his opponents under the condition of Rika consuming a body part of them. Hence, Rika Orimoto becomes a creature he can summon for 5 minutes by wearing the wedding ring from his childhood love promise, and that will increase Yuta's cursed energy amount and efficiency, as well as a combat partner in the shape of a powerful creature.

"Authentic Mutual Love" is his domain expansion, a cross-structures field full of katanas, which when picked will allow Yuta to get a random previously copied technique which has the sure-hit effect, and after its usage will vanish.

Maki Zenin

Maki Zenin is one of the main secondary characters in the Jujutsu Kaisen manga/anime.

She is introduced as a member of the Zenin clan, who continuously got neglected and harassed by her own family for lacking a cursed technique and the capacity to see cursed spirits without her especial lenses, therefore having deep hatred and an urgency for vengeance to them for that.
Despite her limitations, she is more athletic than most sorcerers, and has a proficient management of hand weaponry.

During the first half of the series, Maki has to fight through ordinary methods, but after her final clash with the Zenin clan, she manages to get rid of every apex of cursed energy inside of her, in exchange for and uncomparable strength and sharpening of her senses in the shape of a "heavenly restriction", making her even stronger than most cursed energy users.

Panda

"Panda" is a secondary character in the Jujutsu Kaisen manga/anime.

He is introduced as a normal student of Tokyo Jujutsu high - despite his appearance of a panda - but is later explained to be a cursed corpse, artificially created by Masamichi Yaga - the director of the Tokyo Jujutsu High - with the use of cursed energy and three "soul cores": the panda, the gorilla, and the triceratops; who are presented as a family of three siblings.

Since he isn't a proper living organism, his injuries are easier to heal, as he seems to be filled of cotton.
His panda form is the one he usually presents, and when that form is lethaly damaged, he swifts into the gorilla form, and when this one is damaged, he swifts into the triceratops form. The gorilla form is as powerful as a professional sorcerer, and his triceratops form might be even stronger than that.

Toge Inumaki

Toge Inumaki is a secondary character in the Jujutsu Kaisen manga/anime.

He is introduced as a quiet boy who only speaks onigiri ingredients, no coherent phrases, something that the people close to him seem to perfectly understand, as if he was normally speaking.

His cursed technique is praised as a mortal ability known as "Cursed Speech", since it allows him to attack his enemies with commands in the shape of words. Phrases like "sleep", "stay still", or "die", allow him to force the enemy to attend to that command, which effectiveness is determined by the strength of the command and the strength of the enemy.
For this reason, Inumaki only speaks onigiri ingredients, in order to prevent accidental activations of his technique.

Kinji Hakari

Kinji Hakari is a secondary character in the Jujutsu Kaisen manga.

Hakari is introduced as one of the two third year students at Tokyo, both of them who got suspended for improper behaviour regarding cursed energy usage.
When he physically appears in the story, he is managing an illegal sorcerers fighting club, and is approached by Itadori, Fushiguro and Panda to help them restore order in Japan after the Shibuya Incident.

Hakari's cursed technique, "Private Pure Love Train", activates in direct relation with his domain expansion, "Idle Death Gamble".
His intention when using his technique is activating a "jackpot" in his domain - something similar to a gamble - which has the small chance of 1/239 from happening. However, if happening, allows Hakari to have unlimited cursed technique and automatic reverse cursed technique - healing himself - for 4 minutes and 11 seconds.
This technique might be unefficient for the rest of the characters, but throughout the story, Hakari manages to have an unbelievably good luck that allows his cursed technique to be highly useful.

Kirara Hoshi

Kirara Hoshi is a secondary character in the Jujutsu Kaisen manga.

Kirara is introduced as one of the two third year students at Tokyo, both of them who got suspended for improper behaviour regarding cursed energy usage.
Despite the appearance, it is emphazised that Kirara is a male, and he seems to be in a relationship with Hakari, serving as his right-hand in the management of the illegal sorcerers fighting club Hakari operates.

His cursed technique has the name of "Love Rendezvous", and its execution has a direct relationship with the Southern Cross constellation.
Kirara has the capacity to mark cursed energy cores (persons, weapons, objects, creatures, etc) with symbols regarding the five stars of the constellation, and defines a path the victim has to cross in order to approach Kirara. In other words, a puzzle just to try to get him.

Arata Nitta

Arata Nitta is a secondary character in the Jujutsu Kaisen manga/anime.

Nitta has a very brief intervention in the series, just fully appearing during the end of the Shibuya Incident arch as the only first year student of the Kyoto Jujutsu High, and later during the Shinjuku Inhuman Showdown far away from the combat arena, as a supportive healer.

His technique is similar to a painkiller, as his ability consists in stopping pre-existent injuries from getting worse, but not healing them or preventing new ones from generating.

Mai Zenin

Mai Zenin is a secondary character in the Jujutsu Kaisen manga/anime.

Mai is introduced as the twin sister of Maki Zenin, who differently to her sister, does have a cursed technique and was able to find her place in the Zenin clan.
She appears to be very narcissistic and cruel, but later on, she is shown to be vulnerable and insecure, especially when she compares herself to Maki, as she sees her as a true sorcerer, recognizing that she was jealous of her all the time, and that her life without her in the Zenin clan made no sense, claiming the only reason she felt safe there was because Maki was protecting her by receiving all the insults and complains, leaving Mai untouched.
During the Perfect Preparation arch, her intervention allows Maki to obtain the "heavenly restriction" to become one of the strongest sorcerers.

Mai has one of the most potential-dense technique of the series, "Construction", as she is able to create bullets out of thin air. However, her cursed energy capacity isn't enough to correctly sustain this technique, as she only manages to create one bullet per day, and with a very distressful backslash.

Kokichi Muta/Mechamaru

Kokichi Muta, a.k.a Mechamaru, is a secondary character in the Jujutsu Kaisen manga/anime.

Muta is introduced as an arrogant jujutsu student from Kyoto, who is apparently antagonic to characters like Panda, since a "marionette" like him manages to have a normal life, while Muta just can't.
Muta was born with an ill and weak body, losing most of his limbs, confined to a bath tub for life, and with bandages covering all his skin to protect him from the sunlight, all due to a "heavenly restriction" existent since his birth. He enters into an agreement with the villains of the story to get a healthy body, and, after that, and during the Shibuya Incident arch, he serves as the communication between many sorcerers, assisting them with the logistics during the battle.

Muta's technique, "Puppet Manipulation", allows him to control self-made puppets as if they were extensions of himself, being capable of hearing, recording, speaking, use cursed energy, etc. In this sense, his predilect marionette is known as "Ultimate Mechamaru", a puppet capable of facing professional sorcerers, and his definitive puppet, "Ultimate Mechamaru-Mode: Absolute" allows him to display amounts of cursed energy comparable to the strongest sorcerers in the series.
He also manages to accumulate gigantic amounts of cursed energy throughout his almost 18 years of life, which he saved in his definitive puppet for his final battle, as well as the usage of "simple domain" tactics.

Kasumi Miwa

Kasumi Miwa is a secondary character in the Jujutsu Kaisen manga/anime.

Miwa is introduced as an assistant of the Kyoto faculty director during an interview with Satoru Gojo, who she highly idolizes and sparks joy for.
During her appearances, she continously blames herself for her lack of skill as a sorcerer, insistently calling herself "useless" and "weak", something that she bases in her natural lack of an innate cursed technique and the overall better performance of her colleagues.

Despite her lack of an innate technique, she manages to have an experienced use of katanas, as well as a decent usage of simple domains.

Aoi Todo

Aoi Todo is a secondary character in the Jujutsu Kaisen manga/anime.

He is introduced as one of the most proficient student sorcerers, and possibly the strongest sorcerer established in Kyoto. However, his attitude is highly volatile, hostile, and even toxic, as in his first appearance he decides to harass and violently attack Fushiguro for no apparent reason other than testing his skill.
Despite this, when meeting Itadori during the Kyoto Goodwill Event arch, he develops a strong emotional bond with him just for the common liking of certain type of women, to the point where Todo's brain creates fake memories of them attending to the same middle school as best friends.
Despite his natural toxic and disgusting personality, he keeps an unique bond and loyalty to Itadori at all times.

"Boogie Woogie" is the name of Todo's technique, which allows him to teleport cursed energy cores by swapping their position with other cores (weapons, persons, curses, etc). By doing this, he has shown to basically counter any other cursed technique, something that comes especially handy when fighting along Itadori, creating an unbreachable vortex of combos for the rival. Apart from that, Todo has a decent knowledge in simple domains as well.

Momo Nishimiya

Momo Nishimiya is a secondary character in the Jujutsu Kaisen manga/anime.

Nishimiya is introduced as one of the students from the Kyoto Jujutsu High. She is praised as a valuable element of land recognition, communication, and support during the hardships the characters endure.

She always carries with her a broom, with which she flies and launches wind slashes as part of her cursed technique, making her a direct counter to land enemies. However, her destructive power tends to be far behind the appearing menaces throughout the story, therefore she keeps her performance for the capacities explained previously.

Noritoshi Kamo

Noritoshi Kamo is a secondary character in the Jujutsu Kaisen manga/anime.

Kamo is introduced as the arrogant heir to being the head of the Kamo clan as part of the Kyoto Jujutsu High.

His cursed technique, "Blood Manipulation", is an inheritance of his clan, a technique through which he can manipulate his blood, allowing him a big arsenal of abilities such as slicing, explosions, manipulation of objects from afar, etc.
Despite the praising of his technique, he later tends to get far behind other Blood Manipulation users, and usually gets excluded from big fights.
